AI Assistant - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

1. About the Application

AI Assistant is an experimental Microsoft Teams application designed to support AI-powered conversations, image processing, and OneDrive integration.

2. Information Processed

The application may process messages, images, audio recordings, files, and user identifiers submitted through Microsoft Teams.

3. Third-Party Services

Information may be transmitted to Microsoft, Make.com, and OpenAI to provide the requested functionality, including AI processing and file storage.

4. Data Storage

Data may be stored or logged by the connected services according to their applicable settings, retention policies, and service agreements.

5. Security

The application is currently experimental. Users should avoid submitting sensitive or confidential information during testing.

6. Privacy Questions

Contact the administrator who provided access to this application regarding privacy questions or requests concerning personal information.
