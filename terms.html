AI Assistant - Terms of Use

Last updated: November 20, 2026

1. Purpose

AI Assistant is an experimental application for Microsoft Teams, designed to provide AI-powered conversations and assistance.

2. Available Features

The application is being developed to support text conversations, image generation, image editing, voice messages, and OneDrive integration. Features may be unavailable during testing.

3. Acceptable Use

Users must only submit information and files they are authorized to process. Users should avoid sharing sensitive or confidential data unless explicitly approved.

4. AI-Generated Content

AI-generated responses and images may contain inaccuracies. Users are responsible for reviewing outputs before relying on or sharing them.

5. Third-Party Services

The application may integrate with Microsoft Teams, Make.com, OpenAI, and Microsoft OneDrive. Their applicable service terms may also apply.

6. Availability

This application is experimental. Features may change or become temporarily unavailable.

7. Contact

Questions about the application can be directed to the application administrator.
